How sizes differ between countries

US, UK and EU brands label the same body measurements differently. Women's sizes step up in twos, men's shirts use collar size, and kids' sizes follow age or height.

These charts show typical equivalents. Brands vary, so check the maker's size chart when you are between sizes or buying fitted clothes.
